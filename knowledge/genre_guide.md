# Genre guide

One section per genre. "In catalog" says whether VibeMatch has songs in that genre. "Related" lists catalog genres that sound close enough to earn partial credit.

## pop
Aliases: pop music, top 40, mainstream, radio hits
In catalog: yes
Related: indie pop, latin, edm
Typical energy: 0.7-0.9
Catchy hooks, verse-chorus songs built for the radio. Upbeat and polished; happy pop and intense workout pop both live here.

## indie pop
Aliases: indie, bedroom pop, alt pop, alternative pop
In catalog: yes
Related: pop, synthwave, folk
Typical energy: 0.5-0.8
Pop songwriting with a lighter, homemade or jangly sound. Often bright and happy, close to mainstream pop in feel.

## lofi
Aliases: lo-fi, lo fi, lofi hip hop, lo-fi hip hop, chillhop, study beats, beats to study to
In catalog: yes
Related: ambient, jazz, hip-hop
Typical energy: 0.3-0.45
Dusty, mellow beats with soft piano or guitar loops and vinyl crackle. Made for studying, focus and late-night work.

## rock
Aliases: rock music, hard rock, classic rock, alt rock, alternative rock, alternative
In catalog: yes
Related: metal, indie pop, blues
Typical energy: 0.7-0.95
Guitar-driven songs with live drums. Ranges from anthemic to aggressive; the catalog's rock is fast and intense.

## metal
Aliases: heavy metal, metalcore, thrash, death metal, heavy music
In catalog: yes
Related: rock
Typical energy: 0.9-1.0
Distorted guitars, pounding drums and shouted or growled vocals. The most aggressive genre in the catalog.

## ambient
Aliases: ambient music, atmospheric, soundscape, drone, space music
In catalog: yes
Related: lofi, classical
Typical energy: 0.1-0.35
Slow, textured sound with little or no beat. Good for sleep, meditation and deep focus.

## jazz
Aliases: smooth jazz, cafe jazz, coffee shop jazz, bebop, swing
In catalog: yes
Related: lofi, blues, r&b
Typical energy: 0.3-0.6
Acoustic instruments, saxophone and piano, relaxed swing. Warm and cozy.

## synthwave
Aliases: retrowave, outrun, 80s synth, darksynth, synth pop, synthpop
In catalog: yes
Related: edm, indie pop, pop
Typical energy: 0.6-0.8
Neon 1980s-style synthesizers and drum machines. Nostalgic and nocturnal, made for night drives.

## hip-hop
Aliases: hip hop, rap, hiphop, trap, boom bap, drill
In catalog: yes
Related: r&b, lofi
Typical energy: 0.6-0.85
Rhythmic rapped vocals over beats. Confident and swaggering; often explicit.

## edm
Aliases: electronic, electronic dance music, dance, house, techno, trance, dubstep, rave, club
In catalog: yes
Related: pop, synthwave
Typical energy: 0.85-1.0
Big synth drops and four-on-the-floor kick drums. Euphoric and built for clubs, festivals and workouts.

## classical
Aliases: orchestral, piano, symphony, opera, baroque, neoclassical
In catalog: yes
Related: ambient
Typical energy: 0.1-0.4
Composed instrumental music for piano or orchestra. Calm to dramatic; very instrumental.

## country
Aliases: country music, americana, bluegrass, nashville
In catalog: yes
Related: folk, blues
Typical energy: 0.4-0.7
Story-driven songs with acoustic guitar, fiddle and steel guitar. Warm, nostalgic, open-road feel.

## r&b
Aliases: rnb, r and b, rhythm and blues, soul, neo soul, slow jams
In catalog: yes
Related: hip-hop, jazz, pop
Typical energy: 0.4-0.7
Smooth vocals over grooving beats. Romantic and warm.

## folk
Aliases: folk music, acoustic, singer-songwriter, indie folk
In catalog: yes
Related: country, indie pop
Typical energy: 0.2-0.5
Acoustic guitar and voice, intimate and lyrical. Often tender or sad.

## reggae
Aliases: dub, roots reggae, ska, dancehall
In catalog: yes
Related: latin
Typical energy: 0.4-0.6
Off-beat guitar skank and deep bass. Relaxed, sunny and carefree.

## latin
Aliases: reggaeton, salsa, bachata, latin pop, cumbia, bossa nova
In catalog: yes
Related: pop, reggae
Typical energy: 0.6-0.9
Latin rhythms, percussion and dance grooves. Playful and sunny.

## blues
Aliases: blues music, delta blues, electric blues
In catalog: yes
Related: jazz, rock, country
Typical energy: 0.3-0.6
Twelve-bar progressions, bent guitar notes and soulful vocals. Melancholic and rainy.

## k-pop
Aliases: kpop, korean pop
In catalog: no
Related: pop, edm, indie pop
Typical energy: 0.7-0.95
Highly produced Korean pop with dance breaks. Not in the catalog; the closest songs are upbeat pop and EDM.

## j-pop
Aliases: jpop, japanese pop, anime songs
In catalog: no
Related: pop, indie pop
Typical energy: 0.6-0.9
Japanese pop. Not in the catalog; the closest songs are bright pop and indie pop.

## punk
Aliases: punk rock, pop punk, hardcore punk, emo
In catalog: no
Related: rock, metal
Typical energy: 0.85-1.0
Short, fast, loud guitar songs. Not in the catalog; the closest songs are rock and metal.

## afrobeats
Aliases: afrobeat, afropop, amapiano
In catalog: no
Related: latin, hip-hop, reggae
Typical energy: 0.6-0.85
West African pop rhythms with syncopated percussion. Not in the catalog; the closest songs are latin, hip-hop and reggae.

## gospel
Aliases: gospel music, worship, christian music
In catalog: no
Related: r&b, blues
Typical energy: 0.4-0.8
Choir-led, uplifting spiritual music. Not in the catalog; the closest songs are r&b and blues.
