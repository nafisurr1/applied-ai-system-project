# Song notes

Short liner notes for every song in data/songs.csv: instruments, production and lyrical themes that the numeric features do not capture. Section titles start with the song id.

## 1. Sunrise City — Neon Echo
Bright synth-pop with handclaps, a whistled hook and a big singalong chorus about summer mornings in the city. Clean lyrics. Feels like a sunny commute.

## 2. Midnight Coding — LoRoom
Mellow lofi beat with a muted Rhodes piano loop, soft vinyl crackle and keyboard-typing sounds. Fully instrumental. Made for late-night work sessions.

## 3. Storm Runner — Voltline
Fast rock track with distorted twin guitars, pounding live drums and a shouted chorus about outrunning a storm. Clean lyrics. Aggressive and driving.

## 4. Library Rain — Paper Lanterns
Gentle lofi loop of rain on windows, a nylon-string guitar and a slow, dusty drum beat. Instrumental. Cozy and calm, ideal for reading.

## 5. Gym Hero — Max Pulse
Pumped-up workout pop with booming 808 bass, chanted vocals and a countdown drop. Explicit lyrics about grinding and winning. Built for lifting.

## 6. Spacewalk Thoughts — Orbit Bloom
Weightless ambient piece with long synth pads, distant piano notes and no drums. Instrumental. Sounds like floating in space; good for sleep.

## 7. Coffee Shop Stories — Slow Stereo
Relaxed acoustic jazz with brushed drums, upright bass and a warm saxophone melody. Mostly instrumental. Evokes a rainy afternoon in a cafe.

## 8. Night Drive Loop — Neon Echo
Retro synthwave with arpeggiated 1980s synths, gated drums and a neon city-at-night mood. Mostly instrumental. Nostalgic and nocturnal.

## 9. Focus Flow — LoRoom
Steady lofi beat with soft piano chords and a gentle bassline that never changes tempo. Instrumental. Designed to keep you focused.

## 10. Rooftop Lights — Indigo Parade
Jangly indie pop with chiming guitars, a bouncy bassline and a carefree chorus about watching city lights from a rooftop. Clean lyrics.

## 11. Concrete Crown — Kilo Verse
Confident hip-hop over a hard trap beat with rattling hi-hats; boastful verses about coming up from nothing. Explicit lyrics.

## 12. Laser Bloom — Pulse Theory
Euphoric festival EDM with a soaring synth lead, a long build and a massive drop. Mostly instrumental. Peak-energy club and workout track.

## 13. Moonlit Sonata Redux — Elena Varga
Solo piano reimagining of a classical sonata, slow and melancholic with soft room reverb. Instrumental. Calm and dreamy.

## 14. Dusty Highway — Cal Turner
Country road song with acoustic guitar, pedal steel and a fiddle solo; lyrics about driving home through the 1990s countryside. Clean lyrics. Warm and nostalgic.

## 15. Velvet Hours — Nia Rose
Smooth r&b slow jam with silky vocals, warm electric piano and a gentle groove; a love song. Clean lyrics. Romantic.

## 16. Iron Verdict — Grave Signal
Heavy metal with down-tuned guitars, double-kick drums and growled vocals about rebellion. Explicit lyrics. Dark and aggressive.

## 17. Paper Boats — Willow & Pine
Quiet indie folk with fingerpicked acoustic guitar and a soft duet about a friendship drifting apart. Clean lyrics. Tender and sad.

## 18. Island Time — Sunny Dub
Laid-back reggae with off-beat guitar, melodica and deep bass; lyrics about slowing down on a beach. Clean lyrics. Sunny and carefree.

## 19. Fuego Lento — La Marea
Playful latin pop with congas, a flirty Spanish-language vocal and a reggaeton-style groove. Clean lyrics. Great for dancing.

## 20. Rainy Day Blues — Hollow Creek
Slow electric blues with a weeping slide guitar and rain sound effects; lyrics about a lonely rainy night. Clean lyrics. Melancholic.
